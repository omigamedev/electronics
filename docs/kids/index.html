Sparky's Circuit Adventure

Hi! I'm Sparky, a tiny electron. Come on a journey through wires, bulbs, magnets and robots with me! Tap a bubble to start a level.

👋 For grown-ups

Each level takes 10–15 minutes. Tap 🔊 to have the page read aloud. Most levels end with a "Try it at home" activity using a battery, foil, a bulb or LED, and a magnet. Never let kids play with wall sockets or lithium batteries. The levels gently follow the same path as the grown-up book (Sparks & Circuits), so you can learn together.
