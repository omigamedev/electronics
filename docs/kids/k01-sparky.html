  1. 🍦melted ice cream
  2. ⚡tiny Sparkies moving
  3. 💨wind
Electricity is lots and lots of tiny electrons moving together.

🎈 The magic balloon

⚡Watch this trick! When you rub a balloon on hair, some Sparkies hop from the hair onto the balloon.

👆 Tap the rub button again and again. What does the hair do?

Rub the balloon until the hair stands up!
The balloon got extra Sparkies. The hair lost some. Now they pull on each other, so the hair lifts up toward the balloon! This is called static electricity.

🤗 Pull or push?

⚡Here's a secret. Things with extra Sparkies and things with missing Sparkies pull together. Two things that both have extra Sparkies push apart!
The balloon has extra Sparkies. The hair is missing some. What happens?
  1. 🤗they pull together
  2. 🙅they push apart
Opposites pull together, like best friends giving a hug.
Two balloons. Both have extra Sparkies. What happens when they come close?
  1. 🤗they pull together
  2. 🙅they push apart
Both balloons are the same kind. Same kinds push away.
Same kinds push apart. Hang two rubbed balloons on strings and watch them move away from each other!

⚠️ Sparky's rules

⚡A balloon has just a little electricity. But the holes in the wall, called sockets, have a giant push! It can hurt you very badly. So I have some rules.
🔌🚫✋
Help Sparky! Tap a card, then tap the right box.
✅ Safe
🚫 Never!
  • 🎈 Rub a balloon
  • 🔋 Play with an AA battery with a grown-up
  • 🗣️ Tell a grown-up about a broken wire
  • 🔌 Poke things in a wall socket
  • 💧 Touch plugs with wet hands
  • 🪁 Fly a kite near power lines
  • ✂️ Cut or chew a cable
Wall sockets, plugs and big power lines are only for grown-ups. Water and electricity never mix!
Small batteries and balloons are fun. Wall sockets, power lines and broken cables have a huge push. Only grown-ups touch those!

⛈️ The biggest spark

⚡Lightning is a giant spark! Lots and lots of Sparkies jump from a cloud to the ground. It is super strong.
☁️⚡🌳
Thunder and lightning! Where should you go?
  1. 🏠inside a house
  2. 🌳under a tall tree
  3. ⛳in an open field
Lightning likes tall things and open spaces.
Go inside a building (or a car) and wait for the storm to pass. "When thunder roars, go indoors!"

🏠 Try it at home (with a grown-up)

⚡Let's do real static magic!

You need: 🎈 a balloon and ✂️ tiny bits of paper.

  1. Rub the balloon on your hair or a wool sweater.
  2. Hold it over the paper bits. Do they jump up?
  3. Rub it again and hold it near your hair. Look in a mirror!
  4. Turn on a tap to make a very thin stream of water. Bring the rubbed balloon close. The water bends!
⚠️ Sparky's rule

Only play with balloons and small batteries. Never touch wall sockets.

👨‍👩‍👧 For grown-ups
Rubbing transfers electrons between materials (the triboelectric effect): the rubber balloon ends up negative and the hair positive. Neutral paper and water are attracted because the balloon's field polarises them. Mains is dangerous because its high voltage drives a harmful current through the body's resistance.
