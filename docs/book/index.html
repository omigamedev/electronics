Sparks & Circuits

Electronics for Developers

A path from the physics of current to your own robot controller PCB, built for people who already think in code. Follow the chapters in order; each builds on the last.

📚 Must-have companions
  • Practical Electronics for Inventors, Scherz & Monk: daily reference
  • The Art of Electronics (3rd ed.), Horowitz & Hill, with Learning the Art of Electronics (Hayes & Horowitz) for labs
  • Making Embedded Systems (2nd ed.), Elecia White
  • There Are No Electrons, Kenn Amdahl: intuition
  • Make: Electronics, Charles Platt: hands-on
  • Lectures: MIT 6.002 (Agarwal), MIT 8.02 (Lewin), Feynman Lectures Vol. II; YouTube: Ben Eater, EEVblog, Phil's Lab, GreatScott!, Brian Douglas
